Design Tips for The Perfect Custom Box

Designing the perfect custom boxes requires attention to detail. When designing your boxes, there are several steps that you must follow to achieve the best result possible:

  • Understand Your Product: Start by considering the product's size, weight, shape, and protection needs before designing the box.
  • Choose the Right Material: Select a packaging material that provides enough protection and suits your product.
  • Add Your Brand Elements: Use your logo, brand colors, artwork, and other details to make the packaging easy to recognize.
  • Consider Sustainability: Choose recyclable, reusable, or responsibly sourced packaging options when they fit your product and business needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

We've answered all the common questions you may have before ordering custom boxes.

What are custom boxes?

Custom boxes are specialized containers that help to advertise, protect, and ship your products effectively. You can print them with your branded artwork and custom logo to strengthen your brand identity.

How do I know which box size I need for my custom box?

To determine your box size, measure your product from the widest side for the width, the longest side for the length, and the tallest side for the height. Once you have these measurements, add a few inches (e.g., 0.25 inches) to allow room for fit and any packaging fillers. You can also read our full guide on measuring your box dimensions for a better understanding.

What are the most popular types of custom boxes?

The popular and common types of custom boxes are as follows: folding cartons for retail products, mailer boxes for shipping ecommerce products, and rigid boxes for showcasing and protecting luxury or premium items.

Which custom box is perfect for shipping ecommerce items?

For shipping ecommerce items, custom boxes made from corrugated materials are the best choice. The most popular choice for shipping ecommerce products is a mailer box, as it is made from corrugated materials and can protect your product while leaving a lasting impression with each delivery.

Can I order a sample of my custom box?

Absolutely. You can order a sample of your custom box to check the material quality, printing, finishing, and size. If you are satisfied with the result, we will start manufacturing the rest of your order.

How does ordering custom boxes benefit my small business?

Ordering custom boxes from Pack Legacy can benefit your small business. We offer no minimum order, no setup fees, and no die-plate charges, making it cost-effective and easy for small businesses to order from us.

Which sides are printable on my box?

Printing sides of your box is fully customizable. You can choose where you want your artwork printed, such as the inside, outside, or both sides. If you need a plain custom box, we can provide that too.

Do you also offer custom box inserts?

Yes. We do offer custom box inserts, such as EVA foam inserts, cardboard inserts, corrugated box dividers, and packaging card inserts. You can customize your box insert size as per your product size requirements.

How much do custom boxes cost?

The pricing of custom boxes depends upon various factors, including size, material, style, and design complexity. Also, there is no extra charge for any additional color prints.

Can you help me with designing my box artwork?

Yes. We offer dedicated design support to help you with your custom box artwork. Also, if you require a dieline template for your box, please visit our free dieline template page.
